Who We Are

CoreClean Laundry is a professional commercial laundry company based in Nashik, committed to delivering hygienic, reliable, and high-quality laundry solutions for businesses.


We specialize in handling bulk laundry requirements for hospitals, hotels, restaurants, industries, corporate offices, educational institutions, and healthcare organizations using modern equipment and professional processes.


Our objective is to provide exceptional fabric care while ensuring hygiene, timely delivery, and complete customer satisfaction.
